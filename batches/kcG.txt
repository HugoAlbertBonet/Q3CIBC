#                CP (cheapest possible Langevin); 8 = top-8 chains.

# same-seed trials share ~identical weights: clean inference-only A/B.
# Chain params unless varied: first-chain noise 1.0, lr_final 1e-5, poly decay
# 2.0, delta_clip 0.5, again noise 0.5. Efficiency read from eval_time_s.
